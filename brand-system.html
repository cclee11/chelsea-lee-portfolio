color

palette

One fixed navy ink for text on colored surfaces, one light blue for buttons/accents, one warm yellow accent, one gray for strokes/body text, and an off-white that flips with the page background for night mode. Ink, background and accent-warm never change between light and dark — only the stroke/text role and the page background do.

personality

voice & principles

lowercase & warm

body copy and everyday labels stay lowercase and conversational — approachable first, formal never.

caps for structure

only page titles and section headers go into tracked all-caps, so the eye always knows where a new section starts.

tactile, not flat

soft shadows, gentle tilts, and rounded bubbles keep the UI feeling hand-placed rather than templated.
